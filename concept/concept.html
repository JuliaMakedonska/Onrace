Color rules, checked by the build

From concept.md → Color rules. scripts/concept/build_stand.py checks them on every build: rules 1, 2 and 3a stop the build when broken; 3b is reported here and does not block (the current amber is accepted by the designer).

1 · Accent placement · pass

Amber only where you can act or where you are. The build found the accent on 17 selectors, all allowed: the primary button and its hover, .header-action, the primary .state-action, the grayscale screens' one primary (.register-btn, .open-btn, .submit-btn), Apply filters in the sheet, Save in Log result's top bar (amber text: that screen has no tab bar and no other amber), the current tab, and the specimen swatches. It is never on a result, a finish time, a count or a status.

2 · No success color on results · pass

Results are self-reported; Onrace can't verify them. No product screen uses --success: the saved confirmation on Results list is neutral (a surface and a "save" glyph), because a green check next to a race name reads as a "verified" stamp. The build allows success color only on this stand's specimen. The proof line stays Text 2 (attribute d), and even this page's "pass" labels are Text 1, not green.
