Three dark directions for Onrace

Lesson 6, step 3. Each panel uses the same content: a race card from the catalog, one result from the archive with its source line, the primary button, the format chips, and Solar icons. Only the visual decisions change. Choose one; nothing here is a token yet.

All three share these rules: dark with depth (no pure #000), no "black plus one hot accent", no aggressive red, none of the guardrail values, and chips in markup case ("Half marathon"), uppercased by CSS only.

Direction 1 · restrained color · sharp

Chalk on rubber

The look of a functional-fitness floor between heats: warm rubber, chalk, a whiteboard of times. Color appears on two things only, the finish time and the format chip.

Energy source
Type. Race names and finish times are set like arena signage: tall, tight, heavy.

Guess test
Passes. "Sports app" predicts neon on black, Strava orange or HYROX yellow on black. This is warm charcoal, chalk and a pale magnesium blue, with no saturated accent at all. Risk: heavy condensed display type is itself a common move, so it holds only while the palette stays this quiet and there are no hard offset shadows.

Direction 2 · rich color · soft

Format colors

Every format owns a color, the way a race series owns its course markings. The chip sets the card: date tile, chip and Register button all take the race's format color.

Energy source
The format chip. Scrolling the list reads as a run of colors: HYROX apricot, OCR rose, Ultra moss.
Attributes served
achips compete with time btinted date tile cstrongest: own color dsource line uncolored ecolor, no alarm hues
Guess test
Passes. No single sport sets the look, and the six format colors sit on aubergine, not black. The one reference color it could drift into is fitnessracingcalendar's lime, so Ultra is a soft moss, not acid lime, and HYROX is apricot, not yellow.

Direction 3 · middle color · wide type

Floodlight

A night race under floodlights. Everything is held in deep petrol blue, and only the facts are lit: the date and the time. Wide type and a measuring mono instead of tall signage.

Energy source
Contrast. The brightest pixels on screen are always data: the day in floodlight white, the finish time in sodium amber.
Attributes served
astrongest: time is lit bdate in white mono cglacier chip dsource line stays dim econtrast, not hue
Guess test
Passes, closest call. It is not neon on black: the ground is blue, the accent is a warm amber with no glow, and amber appears only on the time and the one action. Risk: amber read quickly could suggest HYROX yellow. HYROX is a lemon ~#FFE600 on black; this is #F5B649 on petrol. If it still reads as HYROX in the browser, the direction fails the test.
