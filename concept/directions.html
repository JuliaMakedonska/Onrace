Three dark directions for Onrace · round 2

Each direction pairs a display face (titles, finish times) with a separate text face, uses a small radius and shows black-and-white photos. The two phone screens use the wireframes' text and structure, restyled. One direction (A) uses round 1 Direction 3's amber exactly, so you can judge the HYROX question next to good type.

Shared: dark with depth (no pure #000), no aggressive red, none of the guardrail values, chips uppercase by CSS only, Solar icons. Round 1 is kept at directions-round-1.html.
