Specimen
Race card, with every state beside it

From DESIGN.md

Race card: --surface, 6px radius, 8px padding, flat. The whole card is one link, photo included. Inside it: a black-and-white photo (grayscale(1) contrast(1.12) brightness(0.85)) at 3:1 with a 4px radius, the file itself pre-cropped to its 3:1 band (684 and 1026 px wide, AVIF / WebP / JPEG, srcset); then the date tile (68px wide, --raised, 4px radius, 12px 4px padding, the day in Schibsted 30px --text-primary over the month and year in 13px --text-secondary); then the race name (17px / 600), the format chip, and the location (14px --text-secondary with a 15px Solar map-point icon).
