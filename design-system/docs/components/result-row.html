From DESIGN.md

Result row: --surface, 6px radius, 12px 16px 16px padding, 8px gaps. A 15px Solar alt-arrow-right in --text-secondary ends the meta row, under the finish time's right edge: the row drills into Result detail. The finish time and the proof line keep the row's full width, so the time sits where it always did and no proof line wraps. Race cards are a collection, so they have none. The race name on the left and the finish time (Schibsted 32px) on the right, sharing a baseline; then the format chip and the date (15px --text-secondary, C3); then the proof line (13px --text-secondary, one 15px Solar link icon, wraps anywhere). With no official result link yet (2026-11-06): the proof line says "Self-reported · no official result link yet" (a caption, so it wraps to two lines if it must), and "Add official result link", a text link, sits under the row's link on the same surface (the row's link takes square bottom corners above it): a link can't hold another.
