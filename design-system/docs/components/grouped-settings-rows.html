From DESIGN.md

Confirm alert: the sheet's glass over the scrim, with its blur and rim, 34px corners [?]. Sign out, the destructive action, is --error (coral) text, in the alert and in its Profile row; Cancel stays --text-primary (HIG Alerts). The pressed segment is --pressed, --text-primary, with a 1px --boundary outline. Race detail uses the same alert for "Couldn’t open the race website", with one action, Dismiss, bold as the default; its body says "Tap Register again". Alert buttons are 17px. Result detail uses it too for "Couldn’t open the official result", with Dismiss and Try again; its body points to Copy link.

From DESIGN.md

Profile: a top-level tab with the large title and the floating tab bar (the current tab its one amber). The initials in an 80px circle on --raised; the name and email; the grouped Account rows on --surface (52px, --grouped-row-min, since 2026-11-07; a Solar chevron after Country and Language), the group footer; Sign out as a grouped row in its own group, as iOS Settings (a full-width button until then): the rows' --surface, 52px (--grouped-row-min, --grouped-row-pad), the text centred in coral at the rows' regular weight (--error, a destructive action), the rows' hover and pressed; its group is not a list, so a screen reader hears one button, not a list of one; its confirm alert with Cancel and Sign out in coral (--destructive).
