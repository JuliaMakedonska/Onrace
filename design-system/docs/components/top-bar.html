Top bar

The bar at the top of every screen below the top level: back or Cancel, the title, the bar action.

Example

Discover

Usage

When to use

A drill-down goes back to Discover, by its name.

See it: Race detail ↗

When not to

A top-level screen leads with the large title, not a back button. Use Large title header.

See it: Race browse ↗

Used as Cancel, Account and Sign in or Create account on Sign in / Sign up; Back and title on Race picker, Reset password and New password; a sheet's bar is the Modal sheet's.

Specimen
Top bar, with every state beside it

From DESIGN.md

Top bar: one element on every screen, header.top-bar; ground glass with a rim, below the top safe area; its text buttons 17px. The back link is a.back, its Solar Linear chevron drawn by the kit (no glyph in the markup). Back (a Solar Linear chevron and the parent's name) and Cancel in --text-primary; Save on Log result in --accent-ink (that screen's one primary); saving, the spinner alone, Cancel locked. The sign-in screens' title is "Account" in both modes: the segmented switch carries the mode, the bar action does it.
