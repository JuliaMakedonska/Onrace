From DESIGN.md

Sheet glass (--graphite-17 at 90%, blurred), 38px top corners [?] with a rim along the top edge, the float shadow, over the scrim. It rests at the large detent only, and slides up over 250ms (--dur-sheet, --ease-out; 0ms under reduced motion). No grabber (one detent, not resizable); a top bar with Cancel (Text 1) on the leading edge, "Filters" (17px / 600) centered, Apply filters (amber text, the sheet's one primary) on the trailing edge. The options are grouped rows on --pressed, the level above the sheet, 44px each with --hairline dividers: Region shows its value in --text-secondary with a chevron and opens the native menu; the race dates are compact pickers, the value in --text-primary on a --raised pill with a small Solar calendar; under the "SPORT TYPE" group heading, seven rows with a neutral --text-primary checkmark at the trailing edge when chosen. Focus moves into the sheet when it opens, Escape closes it and focus returns to Filters.

From DESIGN.md

Log result: a modal sheet over Results: the sheet glass at the large detent, Results receded under the scrim; Cancel (Text 1) on the leading edge of its bar, "Log result", Save in amber text (the one primary). Saving: the spinner alone in Save's place. A grouped form (see Form rows): the catalog row, the race facts (or the linked race's card), the finish time, the official result link, each with its footer. Cancel with anything entered opens the action sheet: two glass blocks at the bottom, 34px corners [?], a 13px Text 2 message ("This result isn’t saved yet."), Discard result in coral, and Keep editing apart below it, 600.

From DESIGN.md

Confirm alert: the sheet's glass over the scrim, with its blur and rim, 34px corners [?]. Sign out, the destructive action, is --error (coral) text, in the alert and in its Profile row; Cancel stays --text-primary (HIG Alerts). The pressed segment is --pressed, --text-primary, with a 1px --boundary outline. Race detail uses the same alert for "Couldn’t open the race website", with one action, Dismiss, bold as the default; its body says "Tap Register again". Alert buttons are 17px. Result detail uses it too for "Couldn’t open the official result", with Dismiss and Try again; its body points to Copy link.
