Spacing and layout

  • Screen: 390px wide (--screen-w), one column, scrolling inside .phone-screen. Content runs under the floating tab bar; 72px of bottom padding (bar + gap + safe area) lets the last item scroll clear of it.
  • The phone screen (.phone-screen, components/screen.css; a frame, not a component): the screen's one scroll area, full height, one column; under a tab bar it pads its end by the bar's clearance (--tabbar-clear), a drill-down stacks its top bar and main and lets the page stop at the action bar. A screen with nothing to focus (a loading state) takes the focus itself, so the keyboard can scroll it, and its ring sits inside its edge (outline-offset the ring's width inward), since outside it would be cut by the screen.
  • List area and map area (.list-area, .map-area): the 16px side gutter of a list of cards or rows (Race browse's List view, Results list) and of the map's panel (Race browse's Map view). The layout is on these classes; the ids #list-view, #map-view and #log-form stay for the page scripts only. A form in a sheet (.sheet-form, Log result) has its own top and bottom room under the sheet's bar.
  • Visually hidden (.visually-hidden): words a screen reader hears that nothing shows: a search field's label, a loading status ("Loading saved races…"), a busy button's label; clipped to nothing (--hidden-size), never display: none.
  • Gutter: 16px (--space-4) on every side of the content.
  • Header: 8px 16px 4px below the top safe area, gap 12px. A top bar floats over it on every tab screen (44px, transparent and empty at rest, see Navigation); on Results list it carries Log result at its trailing edge, level with the title.
  • Controls (Race browse): sticky below the top safe area on ground glass, padding 12px 16px, the glass rim under it. The List/Map track (a capsule, equal segments) sits at the row's end at its full size and never shrinks; the Filters button takes the remaining width and shrinks down to its label, never below it. The bar is never wider than the screen (its grid column is minmax(0, 1fr)). Active filter chips scroll in one row, 12px apart, which fades out over its last 24px; chips never shrink or truncate, only the scroll edge cuts them. "Clear all filters" is pinned 12px after the row, always whole. Checked at 390 and 320 wide with 0–5 filters by scripts/check_overflow.mjs.
  • Count row: padding 12px 16px, count on the left, sort control (Race browse) or nothing (Results list) on the right.
  • Lists: cards and rows stacked with a 12px gap.
  • Race card: an 8px inset; the photo band (3:1, 114px tall at 342px); below it a grid of a 68px date column and the text column, 12px apart. 216px tall.
  • First screen: in the viewer (757px), 2 full race cards clear of the tab bar with 1.7px to spare, and part of a third showing through the glass (2 full + 29% on screen); with two filters on, 1 full + 75% clear. At 390×844: 2 full + 35% clear, 2 full + 10% with two filters on.
  • Tab bar: floats 16px in from the sides and 8px above the bottom safe area; a 64px glass capsule, three equal columns, 44px targets. Its labels stop growing at --size-tab-max (13px [?]), so at 200% text each tab stays inside the 64px bar and its focus ring isn't cut by the screen's bottom edge: iOS tab bar labels don't grow with text size.
  • Filters sheet: slides up over a scrim and rests at the large detent only (the full height less the top safe area and a 12px gap [?]), with a sticky footer (Cancel and Apply filters, 12px apart) above a hairline.
  • Map view: a frame that fills the space below the controls, with 16px gutters. Pins sit on each race's real latitude and longitude; labels that would collide move above or below on a leader line.
  • Targets: 44px minimum (--target). The 32px filter chips and the tertiary link extend to 44px with an invisible tap area.
  • Running text: 65ch maximum (--measure).

Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64.
