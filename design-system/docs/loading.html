Skeleton

Bars in the exact shape of what is loading.

When to use

While races load: bars in the exact shape of the cards.

See it: Race browse · loading ↗

When not to

A save that is under way is the button's busy state, with its spinner, not a skeleton. Use Primary button.

From DESIGN.md

Skeletons (loading): --raised bars with a 4px radius, in the exact shape of the card or row they stand for. The finish-time bar is 32px tall, so nothing shifts. A subtle pulse (opacity to 0.55 and back over 1.6s), still under reduced motion (C6). No spinner, no shimmer.
