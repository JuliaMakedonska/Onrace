Icons

Solar Bold icons, drawn as CSS masks in the color of their text; the small UI glyphs (back, chevrons, disclosure, the search magnifier) are Solar Linear, a designer exception.

DESIGN.md → Overview

Do draw icons as Solar Bold masks in the color of their text: 15px in meta, 18px in buttons and fields, 26px in the tab bar. The small UI glyphs (back, chevrons, disclosure, the search magnifier) are Solar Linear.
