# UI inventory

Lesson 7, step 2, 2026-10-18. Every component in the product, read from all 35 wireframe pages (`wireframes/*.html`: 10 screens, each with its state pages) and `sitemap.md`. Written when two screens were in Podium and eight still grayscale; since step 5 (2026-10-22) all 10 are on the kit, and each later step is recorded in its own section below (the last: Step 7).

**How to read it**
- **Screens:** the screen, then its files. A component counts as repeating when it appears on two or more of the 10 screens; state pages of one screen count once. Single-screen components are under [One-off](#one-off).
- **Groups:** `sitemap.md`'s clusters. **Discovery:** Race browse, Race detail. **Archive:** Results list, Log result, Race picker, Result detail. **Infrastructure:** Sign in / Sign up, Reset password, New password, Profile. **Shared:** components that repeat across two or more groups.
- **States:** what the pages actually show. "Base" is the default page.
- **Photo:** whether the wireframe or the Podium screen puts a photo in it. The Race browse card does, and since step 5 Race detail (the same band, at full content width).
- **Native iOS:** the HIG page from `concept/concept.md` → Native iOS, or "none" when iOS has no counterpart.
- **Gaps:** from `concept/concept.md` → Native iOS → Gap triage. **K** = kit (CSS, steps 3 and 5), **M** = markup pass (between steps 5 and 6), **O** = out of scope. The gaps were measured on the two Podium screens, so a gap is listed for a grayscale screen only where the same markup is there.
- Class names are the wireframes' own, quoted so the kit can find them.
- **Step 3b (2026-10-19)** closed every **K** gap listed here in `ui/kit.css` (`concept/concept.md` → Step 3b). The codes stay for the record; **M** and **O** are unchanged.

File-name shorthand: `race-browse` = `race-browse.html`; `-empty`, `-error`, `-loading`, `-check-inbox` are that screen's state pages.

## States map (Lesson 9, step 3, 2026-11-05)

What each component's States shows on its family page in `design-system/docs/` (and under its specimen on `specimens.html`), read by `scripts/build_kit_showcase.py`. Every component you act on has a row; a component that is only content, a container or a state of another (the field error is the text field's Invalid) has none. The build cuts the named stage of the component's specimen down to the **Keep** part and its ancestors, then shows it At rest, Hover, Pressed, Focus and Disabled, in Dark and in Light (`data-theme-scope`), and Invalid where a stage is named. The states come from the product's own selectors through the stand-ins `.is-hover`, `.is-pressed` and `.is-focus`; Disabled sets `disabled` (or `aria-disabled="true"` on an element that has none).

- **Stage:** the specimen stage, by a part of its label.
- **Keep:** the part kept, the nearest ancestor-or-self of the control (— the control itself).
- **Control:** the element that takes Hover and Pressed (the first match in the stage; in the tab bar, inside its frame).
- **Focus, Disabled:** the element that takes each, inside the control (— the control). `*` before a selector: every match inside Keep.
- **Crop:** what the window shows: `keep` or `control`.
- **Width:** the stage's width in a cell: `s` 160, `m` 210, `l` 330 (never wider than the page).
- **Invalid:** the specimen stage that shows the component invalid, by a part of its label (— none).
