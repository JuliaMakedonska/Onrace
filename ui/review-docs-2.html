The docs, quiet

Lesson 9, 2026-11-05, the same day as the family pages. The docs layer turned light and quiet, like Apple HIG, so the product speaks: one accent, used rarely; neutral headings; no frames round the stages; class names as plain gray monospace. Every component section in one order: its name and what it's for; one specimen, cropped to the component when it is a small part of its stage, with the parts legend directly under it (hover, focus or a tap outlines a part; no numbers, no second copy); When to use and When not to as text with a thin green or coral bar, another component only ever a link by name; the other variants, if any; States; the technical details folded. The section in view is now the one marked in the nav and in "On this page". Every shot: 1280 × 1500 and 390 × 1700. Live: design-system/docs/.

Not a look change to the product: every stage on the family pages computes as the same stage on specimens.html, widths and heights included, both themes, at 1280 and 390 (scripts/check_docs_stages.mjs).
