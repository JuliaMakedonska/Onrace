Lesson 9 · step 3 · component states

2026-11-05. Every component you act on got Hover, Pressed, Focus and Disabled from new semantic roles in design-system/tokens/tokens.css, each with a value in dark and in light. This page shows every state that changed, before (the commit before this step) and after, in both themes: 69 component states and 7 screens. Each state is the real one: :hover, :active and :focus-visible forced by Chrome DevTools on the control and its ancestors, as a pointer or a key does, and Disabled by its real attribute (scripts/review_states.mjs). A theme where the state didn't change shows the after shot, dimmed, marked unchanged. The live states, side by side: each component's States on its family page.

  • One rule for fills: hover one level up, pressed two, for every component, cards included. It ends the Lesson 8 hover drift (a row went up one level, a card two).
  • Hover only under @media (hover: hover); pressed is the state on touch.
  • No state moves an element or changes its size: the primary buttons' 1px press and the cards' hover ring are gone.
  • Disabled is the whole control at --opacity-disabled (40% dark, 38% light), no pointer events; --text-disabled is retired.
  • Focus is the role --focus: white in dark, dark in light, at least 3:1 on every background it sits on. Its values equal the old ring's, so no focus shot changed.
  • The field error (Log result, New password, Profile): no ring and no line on the field; a coral footnote with the small error icon under the field or its group, in place of the hint.
  • The segmented control is a capsule everywhere, also at 200% text (C7). Sign out in the alert is styled by a class, not its id.
