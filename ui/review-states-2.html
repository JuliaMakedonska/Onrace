2026-11-06. Five follow-ups, made through the tokens and components, each in both themes. This page shows every state that changed, before (commit d811d69, the end of step 3) and after, dark and light: 21 component states and 2 screens. Each state is the real one, forced by Chrome DevTools on the control and its ancestors (scripts/review_states.mjs). A theme where the state didn't change shows the after shot, dimmed. A state counts as changed when any computed style of the part changed, so a radius that computes differently but draws the same pixels at this size still shows here.

  • The date tile and the format chip stay one level above their card and move up with it: --raised-hover and --raised-pressed while the card is hovered or pressed (Race card, Result row), so they never merge into the card's state.
  • The pressed amber dims: --accent-pressed and the dark --accent-ink-pressed are --amber-69 #DF9A46, darker than rest, its hue 7° warmer, clear of HYROX yellow under rule 3b on its own (26.6°, ΔE 25.8); the build stops if it isn't. Hover stays one step lighter. The light ink pressed stays --amber-26, so the Top bar's pressed shot changes in dark only.
  • Tab bar icons are chosen by data-tab, not by where the tab links, so the specimens show the real compass, stopwatch and person instead of squares.
  • One capsule rule: the search field, Filters, the action bar's button, the active filter chips, the tab bar and its tabs take --radius-capsule; the three half-height radii are retired. At this text size the shapes draw the same; at 200% text they stay capsules.
  • A field error replaces only its own field's helper: on New password with the passwords not matching, "At least 8 characters." stays; on Profile, the group note "Changes save as you make them…" stays beside a save error.
