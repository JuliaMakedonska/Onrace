# Race Roster — screenshot captured

Captured via Playwright MCP, 2026-09-02: `raceroster-home.png` (full-page homepage, public, no login wall).

**Positioning:** "Manage great events. Organize outstanding events on a secure platform, built by a passionate team that truly cares and understands the industry." Entirely organizer/B2B-facing — the two homepage CTAs are "New to Race Roster? Request a demo" and "Looking to register for an event? ... Use the [search]," confirming athletes only ever land here to complete a registration someone else set up, never to browse/discover races on their own.

**Product suite confirmed:** Registration, Fundraising, Timing tools, Virtual events, CRM, Onsite app — sold to three named roles: Timers, Event organizers, Fundraising coordinators. A "Real stories solving real problems" case study section (e.g. MitoCanada tripling a fundraising goal during a virtual Scotiabank Calgary Marathon) reinforces the B2B-success-story tone.

**Company:** confirmed offices in Sarasota, FL (USA) and London, ON (Canada) — a real, established company, not a small/indie tool.

**Monetization:** a "Pricing" nav item exists but was not opened this pass — exact pricing model **data not confirmed**.

**Relevance to Onrace:** reinforces the original audit's classification — Race Roster is infrastructure event organizers buy, not a competitor on either the discovery or archive half. Its event pages remain a plausible real-world target for Onrace's `official_url`/`registration_url` fields once real races are seeded.
