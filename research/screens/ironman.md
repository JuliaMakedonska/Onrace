# IRONMAN — screenshots captured

Captured via Playwright MCP, 2026-09-02: `ironman-home.png` (homepage) and `ironman-races.png` (the "All Races" browse page, public, no login wall).

**Brand tone:** black background, bold white/red display type — another concrete "dark, bold, high-contrast" reference point for this audience (alongside RoxConnect's black/orange and RoxFit's near-black/violet), reinforcing that this visual language is a real convention across the category, not just this one brief's assumption.

**Homepage:** a hero carousel of upcoming/registration-open races (e.g. "IRONMAN Oman — Muscat, Oman — December 5, 2026 — Registration Now Open"), a personalization touch ("Hi IRONFAN! See personalized recommendations" once signed in), and top-level nav for Races / Pro Series / News / Training / Community / Resources / Shop.

**All Races page — a genuine map-first + faceted-filter combination:** a world map with **clustered pin counts** (confirmed on-screen: 54 in Europe, 35 in the US, 12 near Southeast Asia, 10 in Australia, 8 in the Middle East, 7 in Canada, 6 in South America, 4/2s elsewhere — "161 matches" total), plus a filter bar of Race type / Date / Region / Country / Registration / Course type, and a text search box. This is the clearest real-world precedent found for Onrace's exact planned pattern — map as the primary surface, filters layered on top, clustering used once pin density gets high.

**Relevance to Onrace:** directly strengthens the PATTERNS section's case for map-first + faceted-filter as the right MVP shape — IRONMAN, at a scale of 161 global races, still finds clustering necessary (unlike ocrbase or AllTrails at their current scale), which is a useful data point for when Onrace's own seed catalog might need the same treatment. Its personalization touch ("recommended for you" once logged in) is the same kind of feed-as-a-secondary-layer pattern already noted for Strava's "Popular Races For You" rail — not primary, but a validated secondary enhancement once there's user history to draw on.
