# World Athletics — screenshots captured

Captured via Playwright MCP, 2026-09-02: `worldathletics-home.png` (homepage) and `worldathletics-records.png` (Stats → Toplists, a live results/rankings page, public, no login wall).

**Homepage:** a news/media-led homepage (competitions, athlete profiles, "Our Sport" editorial content) rather than a results tool — the results database sits one level down under "Stats."

**Stats section, confirmed via nav:** Stats Home / Calendar & Results / Toplists / Records / **Send Competition Results** / World Rankings. "Send Competition Results" as a distinct nav item confirms results are submitted by competition organizers/officials, not by athletes — the opposite of a self-reported model.

**Toplists page (100m Women, Senior, 2026) filter panel:** Age Category, Timing (e.g. "Only Electronic"), Areas/Countries, Season, Wind reading, Limit By Country, Gender, Event — a deep, sport-science-grade faceted filter set, far more granular than any race-discovery competitor reviewed (ocrbase, Strava, AllTrails).

**Monetization:** a "World Athletics Partner" sponsor badge (Seiko, official timing partner) is shown prominently in the header, plus a "Shop" nav item for merchandise — governing-body sponsorship + merchandise, not a consumer subscription model.

**Relevance to Onrace:** confirms the original audit's framing — this is the highest-trust-signal reference point (official, federation-verified results) but achieved through an entirely different mechanism (federation-submitted results, not self-report) that isn't replicable at Onrace's scale. Its filter granularity (timing method, wind reading, etc.) is a reminder of how much deeper a single-sport authority can go than Onrace's cross-format MVP will attempt.
