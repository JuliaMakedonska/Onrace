# RoxFit — screenshots captured

Captured via Playwright MCP, 2026-09-02: `roxfit-home.png` (homepage — currently a waitlist teaser for a new "Ultra" feature, not the core product) and `roxfit-how-it-works.png` (full feature walkthrough, public, no login wall).

**Homepage:** "Ultra Is Coming" — an email waitlist for "advanced race intelligence and competition beyond race day." Footer confirms "© 2026 ROXFIT Limited" (a registered company) and iOS/Android store badges. Dark, near-black background with a violet/purple accent — another concrete "bold, dark" brand-tone data point for this audience, alongside RoxConnect's black/orange.

**How It Works page — five feature pillars, each with a phone mockup:**
1. **Discover & Track Races** — browse upcoming HYROX events (a filterable list with region/date), follow any athlete live, see splits/race status/live rankings as a race unfolds.
2. **Build Workouts With HYPE** — an AI training assistant; describe a session in text/voice or upload a photo of a written workout and it builds the session.
3. **Plan Your Race, Split By Split (PACEME)** — turn a goal time into a race plan adjusted for venue, including the ROXZONE; race-day schedule with a "Share race" action.
4. **Sync To Apple Watch & Garmin** — run workouts and race plans phone-free with live targets and pace deltas at every split.
5. **Analyse Every Race** — "Find and link your HYROX results, then compare your splits against the field, race average, age group, or any athlete." Splits / Compare / Share.

**Relevance to Onrace:** this is a materially bigger finding than the original one-line description ("Training + result-sharing app"). RoxFit's "Discover & Track Races" + "Analyse Every Race" pairing means it already combines *both* halves of Onrace's MVP — a filterable race-discovery list and a personal results/comparison view — inside one HYROX-specific app. It's the closest thing found across all 15 competitors to Onrace's actual map+archive combination, just scoped to HYROX only and framed as training-first rather than a cross-format map. The "find and link your HYROX results" phrasing suggests results are pulled from an official database RoxFit maintains (its own "3 million+ analyzed races," per its App Store listing) rather than self-reported with a required link — trust model mechanism **data not confirmed** beyond that phrasing.
