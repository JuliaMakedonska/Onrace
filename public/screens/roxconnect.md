- **Pricing section confirmed visually**: Free ($0, basic profile/browsing/limited messaging) vs. Premium ($11.99/mo, unlimited messaging + advanced filters + priority) — matches the freemium figure already recorded in `../research.md`'s deep-dive table.
- FAQ accordion and testimonial cards close the page before a repeated download CTA — no map, no event calendar, no results feature appears anywhere on the site, consistent with prior finding that RoxConnect is partner-matching, not race discovery or archiving.
