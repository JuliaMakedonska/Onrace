Rule

One breakpoint, and why

16list card 39016race card 39016map ≥ 320

--bp-desktop = 71.75rem = 1148px at the default text size, 2296px at 200%

  • One breakpoint, --bp-desktop (71.75rem, 1148px at the default text size; Lesson 10 step 7, designer, 2026-11-20). Set by the web app's own behavior, not a device: the room for Race browse's two floating cards beside the map at its narrowest, --float-gap + --pane-w + --float-gap + --pane-w + --float-gap + --map-min (16 + 390 + 16 + 390 + 16 + 320). A 1280 or 1366 laptop window gets the desktop, and so does an iPad in landscape at 1180. In rem, so at 200% text it switches at 2296 and the desktop comes later rather than squeezing large text.
  • Why one, not two (a deliberate deviation from the lesson): the lesson asks for exactly two breakpoints, and also says that a breakpoint which changes nothing is redundant. Once the tablet became the phone's, the tablet breakpoint (--bp-tablet, 781px, retired 2026-11-20) changed nothing but caps that max-width does at any width, so it went.
  • Where responsive behavior lives:
    • design-system/tokens/tokens.css: the breakpoint and the width primitives (--bp-desktop, --pane-w, --container-max, --container-max-wide, --form-w, --form-sheet-w, --stack-w, --topnav-h, --float-gap, --measure).
    • design-system/components/: every @media, min-width at --bp-desktop's number only (screen.css, tab-bar.css, split-view.css, modal-sheet.css, action-sheet.css, gate-layout.css, primary-button.css, result-row.css), and every @container, (width < 18rem) only (--stack-w): a component reads its own width, never the window. No screen, pattern or docs page has a media query.
    • design-system/patterns/split-view.html (split-view.js): the list with its detail on a desktop, the choice in the URL.
    • design-system/tokens/desktop.js: what a media query can't do, reading --bp-desktop: a detail page's desktop home, the presenting screen under a form sheet (.sheet-presenter), the Filters popover's modality.
    • scripts/build_sizes.py: every photo's sizes, written from the tokens and the slot its <picture data-slot> fills.
    • The viewer's width switch: Phone 390, Tablet 820, Desktop 1440 (#race-browse?width=desktop).
  • The checks: build_components.py (one breakpoint, every width query at its number and min-width only, every container query at --stack-w); check_overflow.mjs (every state at 390, 320, 820, 1148 and 1440 and at 200% text: no sideways scroll, no wrapped control, no title under a button, no text cut without its "…", each tab screen's bar in the form its width asks for); check_focus.mjs (every focus halo whole at 320, 390, 820 and 1440 and at 200% text); check_docs_stages.mjs (the docs' stages at 1440, 820 and 390); check_kit.py (every photo's sizes is its slot's, from the tokens). The final check, step 7: ui/review-layout.html (every screen and state at 390, 820 and 1440, the defect table, what was superseded and fixed).

Decided

Key decisions, with their references

Panes, never card columns

A wider screen gives the room to a second pane (the map, the chosen race or result), never to a second column of cards (HIG Split views).

2026-11-15 · Full record ↗

Components read their own width

No component reads the window: one that has to change stacks its parts by a container query below --stack-w, so a card looks the same in a phone-width pane as on a phone.

2026-11-17 · Full record ↗

The tablet is the phone's, the desktop a web app

A tablet keeps the phone's layout with its column capped; a desktop is a web app with a top bar, Race browse a full-bleed map with floating cards (references: Strava Maps, Komoot).

2026-11-19 · Full record ↗

Sign-in as a split page

On a desktop the sign-in screens are a full page, the form on the left and a race photo on the right (references: Microsoft Copilot, Dribbble, Midday).

2026-11-20 · Full record ↗

Results list as a list and a card

On a desktop the results sit in a centred container: the list with thumbnails on the left, the chosen result as a card with its photo and stats on the right (references: Strava's activity feed, Komoot).

2026-11-20 · Full record ↗

Forms on a tablet follow iPad

A form on a tablet is never squeezed to a phone's width: the bar spans the screen, the form takes the readable width, the type stays the phone's.

2026-11-20 · Full record ↗

One breakpoint, set by behavior

One breakpoint, where the web app's two cards fit beside the map (1148px), so a 1280 laptop gets the desktop; the tablet breakpoint went, since a breakpoint that changes nothing is redundant.

2026-11-20 · Full record ↗

The desktop on every page

The top bar is on every desktop page; Race detail and Result detail open in Race browse and Results list; Log result and Race picker sit over Results list, dimmed.

2026-11-20 · Full record ↗

Deviations

Deliberate deviations, not defects

  • the tablet behaves like the phone (its own breakpoint retired, its caps max-width alone)
  • no card grid (width goes to panes and capped columns, never more card columns)
  • the desktop a web app, not macOS (a top bar, not a sidebar)
  • the map static (no pan or zoom, framed to the races at each shape
  • the interactive map is deferred)
  • one breakpoint, not two.

Recorded, not defects: DESIGN.md → Width, responsive/width-audit.md, concept/concept.md → Lesson 10.
