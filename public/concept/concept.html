Color rules, checked by the build

From concept.md → Color rules. scripts/concept/build_stand.py checks them on every build: rules 1, 2 and 3a stop the build when broken; 3b is reported here and does not block.

1 · Accent placement · pass

Amber only where you can act or where you are. The build found the accent on 9 selectors, all allowed: the primary button and its hover, .header-action, the primary .state-action, the current tab, and the specimen swatches. It is never on a result, a finish time, a count or a status.

2 · No success color on results · pass

Results are self-reported; Onrace can't verify them. --success appears only on the saved confirmation (and its swatch); no result row has a check icon or the word "verified". The proof line stays Text 2 (attribute d). Even this page's "pass" labels are Text 1, not green.
