Primary persona

The HYROX-First Hybrid Athlete

Why primary: the most, and most varied, independent support in research.md — Sport taxonomy findings, COMPETITORS HARD tier, the differentiation thesis in Findings → decisions, and the product's own HYROX-first seeding priority all point here.

Context

  • Identifies as a "hybrid athlete" in the training-methodology sense — deliberately training strength + endurance together (Alex Viada, The Hybrid Athlete, 2013). (Sport taxonomy findings)
  • Treats HYROX as their anchor race format, marathons/OCR/functional-fitness as adjacent interests — how the existing app ecosystem is structured around this audience. (Sport taxonomy findings)
  • [?] HYPOTHESIS Likely overlaps with the user base of existing HYROX apps (RoxConnect, RoxFit, RoxMatchUp) — but the COMPETITORS matrix only shows who these products are marketed to, not usage data. Reframed as a plausible overlap to validate, not a fact.

Jobs

  • Find upcoming HYROX (and adjacent-format) races to enter. (Sport taxonomy findings; COMPETITORS HARD tier)
  • [?] HYPOTHESIS Track results across HYROX plus other formats in one place — inferred from a market gap (no competitor combines this across formats), not a reported want.

Pains

  • Existing tools split discovery and archiving in two, and every cross-format combination in this space is single-sport-only. (Three shared market patterns #3; CONCLUSIONS gap 6)
  • No single official source covers HYROX + DEKA + marathons + triathlons + ultras together. (Race data availability)

Trust triggers

  • Convinces: a source-link requirement is more transparent than RoxFit/RoxConnect/ocrbase's near-zero verification layers. (COMPETITORS HARD tier; BENCHMARK mechanism #2)
  • [?] HYPOTHESIS Scares off: no peer/social layer means no one to catch a fabricated result — a designed-around product limitation, not a stated fear.

Jobs

Job hierarchy

Two co-equal main jobs, not one main + related — Discovery and Results archiving are triggered by opposite situations (planning ahead vs. after competing) and don't reduce to one another. Related jobs are genuine sub-steps toward one of the two; emotional and social jobs are kept separate.

Why two main jobs, not one ranked "main + related." The earlier single-main-job structure reflected an evidence asymmetry, not a real ranking: Discovery currently has four independent research.md sections behind it; Archiving has one finding cited three times. That's a difference in how much the research documents each job, not in which one matters more.
