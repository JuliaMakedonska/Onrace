Lesson 10, step 3 (2026-11-15). The tab bar component and the Tab screen pattern adapt by width; no screen’s markup changed. Each tab screen before (the commit before this step) and after, at the viewer’s three widths, dark and light; then the tab bar’s states in each form.

  • Phone (under --bp-tablet, 781): the tab bar at the bottom, as before.
  • Tablet (from 781): iPad’s tab bar at the top, icon beside label (HIG Tab bars).
  • Desktop (from --bp-desktop, 1343): a glass sidebar on the left (HIG Sidebars); the large title header narrows to its pane.
  • The same three destinations and the same states from the same roles on the same glass in every form. Drill-downs and sheets keep the phone screen until the panes come.
