State message

.state-message · Race browse, Race detail, Results list, Race picker, Result detail, Profile. One component, the iOS ContentUnavailableView pattern: on a screen it is centered in the free area below the header; a 48px icon on top (muted for empty: magnifier, or archive on Results list; the coral error icon for errors), then the title, the body and the actions, centered. Icons are CSS masks; the markup is the wireframes'. Empty (status) or error (alert); one or two actions in the button hierarchy. It sits directly on the page (designer, 2026-10-22): no outline, frame or surface around it, on any screen. In Race browse's Map view the map frame drops its outline, water and land while it holds a state, and the state has no panel. (The white frames on this page are the showcase's specimen frames, not part of the component.)
