Wide layout

Lesson 10, the layout pass (2026-11-18). One spec for every wide screen, written first in DESIGN.md → Width → The wide layout and applied once, in the split view, the tab bar and sidebar and the screen layout; no screen has a media query. Every screen and state below at 390, 820 and 1440, dark, each measured against the spec.

The spec, in short

  • Panes meet edge to edge at the thin divider (HIG Split views: “Prefer the thin divider style”), no outer margin, no gutter between them, as Mail and Maps.
  • The sidebar is on the same grid (HIG Sidebars: it “appears on the leading side of a view”): full height, edge to edge, the same divider.
  • One top line: every pane starts under the tablet's tab bar row or at the window's top; its first content starts --pane-top (16px) below, the sidebar's first row too.
  • Gutters are the phone's (--space-4) inside each pane; nothing stretches past --container-max.
  • The map fills its pane edge to edge, frames the pins with a margin at the pane's own shape, and pulses while it loads.
  • No empty pane without a reason: Race browse on a desktop is the list and a wide map until a race is chosen; Results list keeps “No result selected”; a list with nothing to choose is one column.
  • Viewer: the tablet icon is wider than the phone's.

Self-check

249 shots (83 states × 3 widths), measured in headless Chrome by scripts/review_layout.mjs: aligned top edges (panes, the large title, the sidebar's first row), the sidebar on the grid, no frame inside a pane, the map edge to edge, no blank or empty pane without a reason, no sideways scroll or pin cut by its map, nothing past --container-max, the list pane's gutter, form sheets centred. 0 with a failure.
