DESIGN.md, from the code

Creative North Star: "Podium"

A graphite stand with one amber light on it. The ground is dark but never black, and it gets lighter in small, even steps from the tab bar up to the raised controls. Type and contrast carry the energy, not color: the screen title and the key numbers (finish time, date day) sit in a heavy display face, and everything else is a quiet text face in sentence case. The amber accent appears once or twice per screen, only where you can act or where you are.

The product is an iPhone-shaped app (390px wide) in Operate mode: athletes scan a calendar of races and pull up their own results as proof. So hierarchy beats decoration. On a race card the date and the place lead and the photo is a black-and-white band, not a poster. On a result row the finish time is the loudest thing. The proof line under it says where the result comes from ("Self-reported · via results.hyrox.com"). It never stamps the result as verified.

What the screens refuse: aggressive "energy" red, pure black with one hot accent, one heavy condensed face for everything, no radius at all, per-format colors, success-green on results, and the artifact pages' values (#0b0c0e, the #ff4d2e family).

Key Characteristics:

  • Dark with depth: five background levels between L* 4.99 and 19.67, all near-neutral (C* ≤ 3.87).
  • One scarce accent, sodium amber #F5B649: the primary action and the current tab, and nothing else.
  • Two faces: Schibsted Grotesk 800 for the title and key numbers, Hanken Grotesk 400/600 for everything else.
  • Small radii: 4px inside, 6px outside in content. Bar chrome is native: the floating tab bar is a capsule, controls inside bars are concentric capsules (C7), the Filters sheet takes the sheet radius (38px [?]), and Profile initials are the one circle.
  • Flat content, glass chrome: the tab bar, the controls bar, the top bar, the action bar and the Filters sheet are translucent graphite with blur (C1). Cards never are. Shadows belong to what floats: the sheet and the tab bar.
  • Neutral metadata: format chips, filter chips (gray capsules), map pins, the saved confirmation and every pressed state stay gray.
  • Black-and-white photos, 3:1: inset in the race card, and at full content width on top of Race detail (the same band).
  • Solar Bold icons, drawn as CSS masks in the color of their text; the small UI glyphs (back, chevrons, disclosure, the search magnifier) are Solar Linear, a designer exception (step 6, 2026-10-25: iOS thins its symbols at small sizes).

Tool

ChatGPT image generation, in the chat (not the API).

Workflow

  1. One conversation for all images, so the look stays consistent.
  2. Image 1 is the style reference. Generate image 1 from the style block plus its scene; for every later image, send the style block plus the next scene and ask ChatGPT to match image 1's style.
  3. The fix for text. When an image comes back with text, numbers or logos, reply in the same conversation: "Remove all text, numbers and logos, keep everything else the same".
  4. Check every image before it's used (the step 4 check):
    • on topic for its format;
    • black and white;
    • no text, race numbers, logos or brand names, checked at full size, including small marks on clothing, shoes, bibs and equipment;
    • the crop works at 3:1 (the race card's photo band).
  5. If it fails on a few small marks: retouch locally (since round 3, 2026-10-21), before regenerating:
    • choose the card's 3:1 crop and focus point first; marks outside the visible band need no retouching;
    • for each mark inside the band: mask it by hand (a small ellipse on the mark), fill the mask from the surrounding fabric or surface (OpenCV Telea inpainting), and add grain matched to the flat fabric around it, feathered at the edge (visuals/incoming/work/tool/retouch.py);
    • verify each patch by its pixel values before and after, not by eye alone, and re-check the whole band at full size afterwards;
    • build a before/after review page and get the designer's OK before any image goes on a card.
  6. If it passes: compress (JPEG, longest side 1200, quality about 74) and save it in visuals/ under its scene name. Then set its focus class on the card (photo--low, --mid, --lower, --center or --high, see ui/kit.css), and check the crop at 390 wide. If it fails, leave it in incoming/ and regenerate.

Overflow fix: the controls bar (designer report, 2026-10-24)

The defect: Race browse -empty at 390 wide, with its three active filters, overflowed. The List/Map control was pushed off-screen (only "List" showed), the third chip was clipped mid-text ("1 Oct – 30 No…"), "Clear all filters" was cut to "Clear", and the screen scrolled sideways.

The cause: .controls is a grid whose single column was auto. An auto column grows to its content's min-content width, and the chips row's min-content width is every chip side by side: 437px with three chips, 953px with five. The column widened, and it carried .control-row (Filters + List/Map) and .chips-bar past the screen with it. The chips row's own overflow-x: auto never got the chance to scroll, because its box was never narrower than its content.

The fix, in the kit (ui/kit.css), no page changed:

  • .controls: grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr). The column is the screen's width, never its content's.
  • .control-row .view-toggle: flex: none. List/Map keeps its full size and never shrinks.
  • .control-row .filters-trigger: flex: 1 1 0; min-width: min-content; white-space: nowrap. Filters takes the remaining width and can shrink, but never below its label ("Filters · 5" stays whole at 320).

  • iOS pattern: a horizontally scrolling row of tokens is the iOS filter-bar idiom, and an action that acts on the whole row belongs on that row, at its trailing end, where it stays put while the tokens scroll. [?] Not checked against a specific iOS 26 screen or Mobbin reference.
