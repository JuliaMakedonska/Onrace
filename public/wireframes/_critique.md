# Wireframe critique — 2026-09-25

A full review of every `wireframes/*.html` file (35 pages, and every `?cause=` variant rendered in a browser), cross-checked against `_conventions.md`, `_screens.md`, `../sitemap.md` and `../flows.md`. It checked six defect classes. Findings are listed by priority, dead ends and missing states first, each with what was wrong and what was fixed.

## How the review was done

- **Style creep:** a script read every page's CSS with comments removed. It checked each color against the gray tokens, flagged hue in any `rgb()`, `box-shadow`/`drop-shadow`/`text-shadow`, every `font-family` and its selector, webfont imports, `<img>`/`url()`, SVGs other than `tab-icon`/`back-chevron`, and emoji or symbol glyphs in visible text.
- **Placeholder text:** it searched visible text for lorem/ipsum, "Heading N", "Label", "Value", "Title", TODO/TBD, "Race Name N", and fake field values.
- **Missing states:** every `Files:` line in `_screens.md` (Passes 1–2) was compared with the files on disk, in both directions.
- **Dead ends and primary actions:** each page and cause was rendered at 390×757, and its visible headings and every visible, enabled link, button and field were listed. Loading pages count as closed when they move on through the flow and keep an exit. Race browse's Map view was checked separately, because it's hidden by default.
- **Not on the map:** each of the 10 screens was checked for its node in `../sitemap.md`'s Screens tree, a `["…"]` node in `../flows.md`, a tree entry and a zone note in `index.html`.
- **Flows:** each end-to-end path was walked in the browser: sign-in gate → archive, the catalog link, sign-up confirmation, Profile and sign-out, and password reset → signing in with the new password. All six Mermaid diagrams were validated with `@mermaid-js/mermaid-cli` 11, and `../ia.html`'s copies were checked byte for byte against `../flows.md`.

## Checked and clean

- **Style creep, apart from #6:** every color is one of the 11 gray tokens. There's no hue, no shadows, no webfonts, no images and no SVGs apart from the tab-bar glyphs and the back chevron. The system font appears only on the tab bar, navigation bars, large titles, and the alert (now allowed).
- **Placeholder text, apart from #7:** there's no lorem ipsum or "Heading 1" anywhere. The screens use real sample races (the 7 upcoming editions in Race browse, the 8 past editions in Race picker), real cities, real archive results with official timing domains, and a real sample profile (Maya Rossi, Italy, English). Race price doesn't appear because the data model has no price field (`../CLAUDE.md` → Data model), not because it was left blank.
- **Missing states:** none. Every state `_screens.md` marks ✓ has its page, and no page exists that `_screens.md` doesn't list (35 of 35).
- **Dead ends, apart from #1, #2 and #8:** every empty state has a way forward (Loosen filters / Clear all filters / Log your first result / Type it in instead / change the search). Every error has a retry or its flow's alternative (Try again / resubmit / Request a new link / Send a new link / Dismiss plus retry), plus the bar or tabs as the give-up exit. Every loading page moves on through the flow and keeps its exit (back bar, Cancel, Close, or tabs).
- **Not on the map, apart from #3 and #4:** all 10 screens are in `../sitemap.md`'s Screens tree and in `../flows.md` as nodes, and every page is in `index.html`'s tree with a zone note.
