From DESIGN.md

Sheet glass (--graphite-17 at 90%, blurred), 38px top corners [?] with a rim along the top edge, the float shadow, over the scrim. It rests at the large detent only, and slides up over 250ms (--dur-sheet, --ease-out; 0ms under reduced motion). No grabber (one detent, not resizable); a top bar with Cancel (Text 1) on the leading edge, "Filters" (17px / 600) centered, Apply (amber text, the sheet's one primary; its accessible name "Apply filters", 2026-11-08) on the trailing edge. The bar's text stops growing at --size-bar-max (23px [?], HIG Body at xxxLarge), as iOS bars stop growing and hand larger sizes to the Large Content Viewer, so at 200% text Cancel, the title and the action still fit a 320 sheet; the title truncates before a button moves. Cancel and the action are text only (designer, 2026-11-08, as a native sheet header; gray capsules from 2026-11-07): Cancel in Text 1, the action in the amber ink, each an invisible 44pt target inset from the corner; hover and pressed change only the text color (--text-hover, --text-pressed; the ink's own --accent-ink-hover, --accent-ink-pressed). The options are grouped rows on --group, the level above the sheet, 44px each with --hairline dividers: Region shows its value in --text-secondary with a chevron and opens the native menu; the race dates are compact pickers, the value in --text-primary on a --raised pill with a small Solar calendar; under the "SPORT TYPE" group heading, seven rows with a neutral --text-primary checkmark at the trailing edge when chosen. Focus moves into the sheet when it opens, Escape closes it and focus returns to Filters.
