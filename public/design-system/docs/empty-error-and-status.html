From DESIGN.md

One component, the iOS ContentUnavailableView pattern (designer, 2026-10-19). Centered horizontally and vertically in the free area below the header and above the floating tab bar: a 48px Solar icon on top (--icon-state), then a 17px / 600 heading, 15px --text-secondary body (65ch max), then actions in the button hierarchy, centered, 12px apart. - Empty: a muted --text-secondary icon that names what's missing: magnifier on Race browse (nothing matches), archive on Results list (the results archive is empty). - Error: Solar danger-circle in --error. Coral means error, and since 2026-10-23 also destructive (Sign out); nothing else turns red. - Icons are CSS masks chosen by context; the markup is the wireframes'. - On the page, never framed (designer, 2026-10-22): no outline, frame or surface around the state, on any screen. In Race browse's Map view, while the map holds an empty or error state, the map frame drops its outline, water and land, and the state has no panel: it looks as it does in List view. On a drill-down (Race detail) it is centered in the area under the top bar, with the 16px gutter.

From DESIGN.md

Form rows: Log result and the sign-in screens are grouped forms, as iOS: a label (15px Text 1) and its field in a 44px row on the rows' level (--surface on the page, --group on a sheet), the value in Text 1, left-aligned; a long value (the official result link) stacks under its label. Pickers sit at the row's trailing edge: a menu value with its chevron, a date on a --raised pill. Editable text values are Text 1; read-only values (Profile's Email) and menu values are Text 2, as iOS Settings. Hints are the group's footer (13px Text 2); an error sits under its row (15px / 600 Text 1 after an 18px coral icon), and the invalid rows take one 2px --error ring (--invalid-ring wide) inside, around each run of adjacent invalid rows, with the hairlines that touch it hidden; focus is the white ring outside, so the two never look alike. A navigation row (Find in race catalog) ends in a Text 2 disclosure chevron; it goes while a race is linked (the card's Unlink changes it). The finish time takes the numeric keypad. Empty required fields hold the bar's confirm in Text disabled until they're filled (Sign in, Create, Send), so an empty submit never reaches the browser's bubble; password rows say "Required". While saving, the whole form is locked in one treatment: every control in Text disabled, the bar's Cancel with it; the help text stays Text 2.

From DESIGN.md

Sign in / Sign up, Reset password, New password (the gates): modals with a ground-glass top bar (Cancel or "‹ Sign in", the centered title) that floats over the page, which scrolls under it. A gate intro in --text-secondary, the Sign in / Create account segmented control (6px in content), fields, one full-width amber submit; busy: the spinner and the present-tense label, the form locked. Small text links (Forgot password?, Reset password again) have 44pt targets. Check your inbox: the address in 600, Send a new link (secondary, a spinner while sending). Full-screen errors (an expired reset or confirmation link) are the empty/error component on the page, not a box. The fields are a grouped form and the confirm action is the bar's trailing button, not a full-width button at the form's end.
