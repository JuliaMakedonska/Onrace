From DESIGN.md

Sort button: one button that flips the order, showing the current one: "Soonest first" in --text-primary 15px / 600, then the 15px Solar sort icon. From DESIGN.md

Result row: --surface, 6px radius, 12px 16px 16px padding, 8px gaps. A 15px Solar alt-arrow-right in --text-secondary ends the meta row, under the finish time's right edge: the row drills into Result detail. The finish time and the proof line keep the row's full width, so the time sits where it always did and no proof line wraps. Race cards are a collection, so they have none. The race name on the left and the finish time (Schibsted 32px) on the right, sharing a baseline; then the format chip and the date (15px --text-secondary, C3); then the proof line (13px --text-secondary, one 15px Solar link icon, wraps anywhere).

From DESIGN.md

Race picker: a screen pushed inside Log result's navigation, as picking a value in iOS Settings (designer, 2026-10-24; a sheet before): full-screen on the page ground, the ground-glass top bar with "‹ Log result" and "Find a race", no Cancel. Under it the search capsule (the page gutter, --boundary), the list intro, race cards on --surface without photos; the linked race carries the neutral checkmark. Tapping a race goes back to Log result with it filled in. Its empty and error states sit on the page; "Type it in instead" is the amber primary on -empty (the state's one action) and the tonal secondary on -error, after Try again.
