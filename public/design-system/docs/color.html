Foundations

Color

A graphite ramp, two text grays, one amber, and a soft coral for errors only.

Primary

  • Sodium Amber (#F5B649, --accent-fill, --accent-ink; primitive --amber-78): --accent-fill is the fill of the screen's one primary button (Log result in the Results list header; Change filters on Race browse -empty; Try again on both -error pages; Apply filters in the Filters sheet) and --accent-ink the icon and label of the current tab and the bar action. On-accent text #131518 is 10.16:1 on it. The current tab label is 10.51:1 on the tab bar.
  • Amber, hover (#FFC46A, --accent-hover): the primary button under the pointer. Never at rest.
  • Amber ink, hover (#FFC46A, --accent-ink-hover): the bar action (Save, Log result) under the pointer. Split from --accent-hover in the same value in dark, apart in light.
  • On Accent (#131518, --on-accent-fill): text and icons on the amber fill. The same primitive as --page, --graphite-7.

Neutral

  • Tab-bar Black (#0F1114, --frame; primitive --graphite-5, L* 4.99): the void behind an open sheet. The tab bar glass (--glass-bar) and the water on the static map (--map-water) are built on the same primitive.
  • Graphite Ground (#131518, --page, L* 6.69): the page, the sticky controls bar, the fields inside the Filters sheet, an unchecked checkbox.
  • Graphite Surface (#1D2024, --surface, L* 12.10): race cards, result rows, the search field, the saved confirmation, the Filters sheet, land on the map.
  • Graphite Raised (#272B30, --raised, L* 17.33): the secondary button, the format chip, the date tile, the List/Map track, map pin labels, skeleton bars.
  • Graphite Selected (#2C3035, --selected; primitive --graphite-20, L* 19.67): a control that is on: the selected List/Map segment, Filters while its sheet is open. Never a hover or a press (those are the state roles, Component states below), never a card. It was --pressed. The same primitive is --group (a group of rows one level above its container: the Filters sheet's rows, the linked race on Log result) and --map-lines (the coastlines and borders on the map).
  • Hairline (#31363C, --hairline): dividers only (above the sheet footer, before "Newest first", between Profile rows). Never a fill. Bars use the glass rim instead.
  • Field Line (#686E75, --boundary): the 1px boundary of inputs, the selected segment's outline, the map frame. 3:1 against --page and --surface.
  • Chalk (#F4F5F6, --text-primary): titles, race names, finish times, date days, body text, links, the dot on a map pin, a checked checkbox. The focus ring has its own role, --focus (Component states). 16.76:1 on --page.
  • Ash (#A3A9B0, --text-secondary): meta (date month and year, location, counts, proof line), placeholder text, inactive tabs, sheet labels, the active filter chip's remove mark. 6.90:1 on --surface.
  • Chip Text (#BFC4CA, --chip-text): the format chip's label. 8.11:1 on --raised.

Glass

  • Tab-bar glass (--glass-bar): --graphite-5 at 85% (--alpha-85) with a 20px blur [?] and a faint Chalk rim at 8% [?]. The floating tab bar and the sticky action bar on Race detail and Result detail, a shade darker than the page so they separate.
  • Ground glass (--glass-ground): --page at 85% with the same blur. Bars that sit on the page (the controls bar, the top bar, the status-bar edge): at rest they match the page; they show only when content runs under them, as a scroll edge effect.
  • Sheet glass (--glass-sheet): --raised at 90% with the blur, over the scrim (C4: the raised level).
  • How the opacities were set: the build composites each glass over the worst case behind it (Text 1, the brightest thing that can scroll under; for the sheet, Text 1 under the scrim), without counting on the blur, and every text pair on it must pass AA. The lowest passing opacities are 81% (tab bar), 82% (ground), 87% (sheet); the values carry a margin. All are above HIG's 35% dimming layer.
  • Reduce Transparency: every glass turns into the solid level it's tinted from.

States

  • Soft Coral (#EE8B84, --error): the one error icon before an error heading, the field error's footnote and its small icon, and the text of a destructive alert action, Sign out. Not the banned aggressive red.
  • Coral Wash (#3A2F31, --error-surface): the error status surface (defined; not shown on these two screens).
  • Scrim (rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), --scrim): behind the open Filters sheet.

--success and --success-bg exist in concept/specimens.css for the stand's specimen only. No product screen uses them.

Light theme: a proof, not a feature Onrace is dark-only (C5). To prove the roles hold a second appearance, design-system/tokens/tokens.css has a :root[data-theme="light"] block that overrides semantic roles only, on ?theme=light. It follows iOS light: the page --graphite-96, white surfaces and rows, --graphite-93 raised and --graphite-89 pressed fills, secondary text --graphite-39. Podium stays: the amber fill is the same #F5B649 with dark text; the ink is --amber-40 (#875206), darker and about 8° warmer, so amber text holds AA on light bars and passes rule 3b without the override; the coral darkens to --coral-40 (#A33A32); photos stay black and white; chips stay neutral. Glass is light and translucent (90–92%), checked over black, the darkest thing that can scroll under it. Decided: the product stays dark-only. Light stays only as proof of the semantic layer: it isn't offered to users and won't be polished. The viewer's Light / Dark control stays for the course, and the build keeps checking light (contrast, glass, color rules 1 and 2, the 3b report), so a component that reads a color primitive is caught (concept/concept.md → The light theme stays a proof, not a feature). Every state in both themes: ui/review-theme.html.

Named Rules The Two Lights Rule. The amber appears at most twice on a screen: the current tab, plus the one primary action if the screen has one. Race browse at rest has only the tab. Log result has no tab bar and no other amber, so its Save, in the top bar, is the screen's one primary and takes amber text, like a native bar button. Every other active state stays neutral: the active filter chip is a gray capsule, the pressed segment is --pressed, a checked checkbox is --text-primary (Text 1, C4). The keyboard focus halo is amber (--focus, since 2026-11-07): it marks where you are (Color rule 1) and shows only while a keyboard moves focus, so it is transient and doesn't count toward the two.

The Stated, Not Stamped Rule. No green, no check icon and no "verified" mark on any result or anywhere on Results list. The saved confirmation is --surface with a gray save glyph.

The Depth of Dark Rule. Every background level has L* between 4.5 and 20 and C* ≤ 6, and each level is at least 1.5 L* above the one below it. #000 and #0B0C0E are out.
