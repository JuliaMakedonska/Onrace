Patterns

Split view

Wider than a phone: a list in a phone-width pane and, beside it, what the list leads to: the map, then a chosen race's detail (Race browse; from the desktop breakpoint list, detail and map), or a chosen result (Results list). The chosen card or row Selected, the choice in the URL. A designer's exception to the three screens (2026-11-17): it stands on two.

At three widths

Since 2026-11-19, Phone, 390, and Tablet, 820: the list, its column capped on a tablet; a card or a row opens its own screen. Desktop, 1440: Results list is two panes under the top bar, the list and the chosen result, "No result selected" until one is chosen; Race browse is the map with floating cards (Width → A new direction). Until 2026-11-19 a tablet split too (list and map, or list and result), and the desktop had a sidebar. The panes meet at the thin divider (--grid-gap); the list scrolls with the screen, the panes beside it stay at the screen's height and the detail scrolls on its own.

When a screen becomes a split view

A split view

when its list leads to something worth seeing while the list stays in view, and the window is wider than a phone. HIG Split views: "selecting an item in the view's primary pane displays the item's contents in the secondary pane"; "Prefer using a split view in a regular — not a compact — environment"; "persistently highlight the current selection". The room goes to a second pane, never to more card columns (responsive/width-audit.md → Decisions, 5).

Single-column

a screen with one thing to read or fill in: a detail opened on its own, a form, Profile, a gate. Its column stops at --container-max and centres. A sheet stays a sheet. On a phone every screen is single-column.

HIG: Split views ↗

How it works

The choice in the URL: choosing a card puts it in the address (?race=…, ?result=…), so a choice can be linked and Back undoes it; the chosen card or row is Selected (aria-current="page"). Clicking a map pin chooses its race too.

One source for the detail: the pane shows the detail screen's own article and action bar, taken from its page (race-detail.html, result-detail.html), so the phone's screen and the pane can't drift apart.

States: the list pane shows the screen's own state (empty, the loading skeleton, error) and the pane beside it keeps "nothing selected"; the map beside an empty or failed list stays a map, without its own message. While a detail loads, the pane shows the detail's skeleton; if it fails, the detail's error shows inside the pane, and Try again retries there.
