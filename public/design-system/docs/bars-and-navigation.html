From DESIGN.md

Tab bar: a floating 64px capsule of tab-bar glass with a faint rim and the float shadow, three tabs (Discover, Results, Profile). Each has a 26px Solar icon (compass, stopwatch, user) over an 11px / 600 label. Inactive: --text-secondary. Current: --accent-ink, icon and label. A tab under the pointer takes --bar-hover behind it, pressed --bar-pressed, a capsule inside the bar.

From DESIGN.md

Sign in / Sign up, Reset password, New password (the gates): modals with a ground-glass top bar (Cancel or "‹ Sign in", the centered title) that floats over the page, which scrolls under it. A gate intro in --text-secondary, the Sign in / Create account segmented control (6px in content), fields, one full-width amber submit; busy: the spinner and the present-tense label, the form locked. Small text links (Forgot password?, Reset password again) have 44pt targets. Check your inbox: the address in 600, Send a new link (secondary, a spinner while sending). Full-screen errors (an expired reset or confirmation link) are the empty/error component on the page, not a box. The fields are a grouped form and the confirm action is the bar's trailing button, not a full-width button at the form's end.

Sticky action bar

The one action of a detail screen, at the bottom, always in reach.

Register on hyrox.com

Opens the race website — registration and payment happen there.

When to use

The one way out of a detail screen: Register on the race's own site.

See it: Race detail ↗

When not to

On a top-level screen the action sits in the state message, not in a bar. Use Primary button.

See it: Results list · empty ↗

Used as Register on hyrox.com on Race detail; Open official result on Result detail; Add official result link on Result detail, no official result link yet.

From DESIGN.md

Action bar (Race detail, Result detail): tab-bar glass with a rim instead of the hairline, above the bottom safe area (C1, designer 2026-10-19). Its caption passes AA over the worst case behind it. Its one button (Register on <domain>, Open official result) is the screen's amber primary and a 22px capsule, as every control inside a bar. On a drill-down the top bar and the action bar are transparent, with no rim, while nothing runs under them (iOS 26 scroll edge): glass once content scrolls under. A link that won't open is the confirm alert.
