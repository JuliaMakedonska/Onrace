Race browse · static map, with every state beside it

From DESIGN.md

A frame in --map-water with a 1px --boundary boundary and a 6px radius. Natural Earth land is masked in --map-land, coastlines and borders in --map-lines. Pins: a 10px --text-primary dot ringed in water color on the real coordinate, and a 22px --raised label (11px / 600, --text-primary, 4px radius) joined by a 1px --text-secondary leader line. Each pin is a 44pt target centred on its label; the dot and the leader take no taps, so where dots nearly touch (Boston, Killington) each label is its own target. Hover lifts the label to --pressed. Each pin's place is map data in ui/map.css, keyed on its data-pin. No accent anywhere on the map. The map has no caption: the wireframe note in its place ("[ static map … ]") is hidden on the working pages (2026-10-23), as are the bracketed notes in its empty, error and loading states. While the map holds an empty or error state, the frame gives way (see Empty and error states). While it loads (aria-busy), the whole map surface pulses with the skeletons (C6: to 0.55 and back over 1.6s; still under reduced motion; designer 2026-10-23).
