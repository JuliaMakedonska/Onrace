Shape

Slightly rounded in content, never sharp. Pills only in bar chrome (C7).

  • 4px (--radius-1), inner shapes: the photo inside a card, the date tile, format chips, List/Map segments, skeleton bars, map pin labels.
  • 6px (--radius-2), outer shapes: cards, result rows, the Race detail photo (it sits on the page), buttons outside bars, the date pills, the Sign in segmented track, the saved confirmation, the map frame.
  • Bar chrome (designer decision 2026-10-19, C7): bar controls are all full capsules, iOS 26 style: radius = half the height., --radius-capsule (999px, clamped by the browser to half the box's own height), so each stays a capsule at 200% text: the floating tab bar and its tabs' state fill, the search field, the Filters button, the List/Map track, the action bar's Register / Open button and the active filter chips. The three half-height radii (--radius-tabbar, --radius-bar-control, --radius-filter-chip) are retired. Content (cards, format chips, tiles, buttons outside bars) keeps 4/6px.
  • The segmented control: a capsule everywhere, in a bar or in content, at any text size: the track and each segment take --radius-capsule (999px, clamped by the browser to half the box's own height), so the List/Map track stays a full capsule at 200% text (47px tall, 23.5px corners) and Sign in / Create account is a capsule too. The other bar capsules followed).
  • Sheet (--radius-sheet, 38px [?]): the top corners of the Filters sheet and the Log result sheet, the native sheet radius (Race picker is a pushed screen with square corners since 2026-10-24); also the receded edge of Results behind the Log result sheet. Replaces the 12px they had. --radius-3 (12px) is now stand panels only. At its own height (.sheet--fit, 2026-11-06): the Add official result link sheet, one field with Cancel and Save, rests as tall as its content (at most the large detent), as iOS sizes a sheet to what it holds; its title names the field, so the field shows only its placeholder "https://…" (its label stays, visually hidden, as its name). Spacing, every sheet (iOS inset grouped lists): 24px between the bar and the first group (--sheet-content-top), 32px between groups (--sheet-group-gap), every row 52px (--grouped-row-min, the 44pt target plus 8; a field or menu fills its row, a stacked field keeps 8px above and below, --grouped-row-pad; since 2026-11-07 the height of every grouped row, not only a sheet's: Form rows), and 32px below the last group plus the bottom safe area (--sheet-content-bottom). The last group drops its gap, so a short sheet has no empty band. The sheet's bar (HIG Sheets): Cancel on the leading edge, the confirming action (Save, Apply filters) on the trailing edge, each a capsule (C7) 44pt tall with a 44pt target (--sheet-bar-h), on --sheet-button (one level above the sheet's glass; a gray capsule in light), hover one level up and pressed two (--sheet-button-hover, --sheet-button-pressed); Cancel in Text 1, the action in the amber ink (Color rule 1), which lightens under the pointer and dims pressed as before. The bar is inset 12px from the top edge and 16px from the sides (--sheet-bar-top, --sheet-bar-side), so no button enters the 38px corner curve; the title is centred between the two and truncates rather than colliding. Disabled (Save waiting for its field) and busy (the spinner alone, Cancel locked) as before. Apple publishes no numbers for these, so all three values are provisional [?].
  • Confirm alert (--radius-alert, 34px [?]): as iOS 26 alerts, provisional like the sheet radius. Its last row of buttons follows the corners, so the inset focus ring and the hover fill do too.
  • Tab bar (--radius-capsule, 32px at its 64px height): the floating capsule.
  • Circles: the map pin dot, the busy spinner, and Profile initials (--radius-avatar, 50%): the one exception to "no circles in content", because iOS avatars are circular.
  • Borders are drawn as 1px inset box-shadows (--border), so they never change a box's size.
DESIGN.md → Shapes

Flat by default. Depth comes from tonal layering: the tab bar (--graphite-5) under the page (--page) under cards (--surface) under raised controls (--raised) under selected fills (--selected). Cards have no shadow and no border at rest, and none in any state: hover takes them one level up, a press two (Component states).

Shadow Vocabulary

  • Float (box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -12px, black at 60%, --float-shadow): what floats over content, the Filters sheet and the floating tab bar. A scrim of black at 60% (--scrim) dims the page behind the sheet.

Named Rules The Float Rule. Only what floats casts a shadow: the sheet and the floating tab bar. Everything else is a tone step.

The Glass Rule. Bars and the sheet are glass (see Colors → Glass); content never is. A card, a row, a chip or a field stays a flat tone.
