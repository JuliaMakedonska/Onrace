# Onrace

An interactive map of races for hybrid athletes, plus a personal archive of official race results with links to source.

Full product brief: [`CLAUDE.md`](./CLAUDE.md). Live app: https://juliamakedonska.github.io/Onrace/

## Wireframes

[`wireframes/`](./wireframes/README.md) holds the full low-fidelity set: 10 screens, 35 pages, one per real state. Browse them live at https://juliamakedonska.github.io/Onrace/wireframes/: an iPhone frame, the screen tree on the left, and zone notes on the right.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`index.html`](./wireframes/index.html) | The browsing shell: device frame, screen tree, zone notes |
| [`_conventions.md`](./wireframes/_conventions.md) | The contract every page follows: fidelity, tokens, iOS chrome, linking, states, test switches |
| [`_screens.md`](./wireframes/_screens.md) | Per-screen state scoping (✓ or — with reasons) and shared sample data |
| [`_critique.md`](./wireframes/_critique.md) | The 2026-09-25 review: defects found and fixed, and what was checked clean |
| [`_wireframe/`](./wireframes/_wireframe/README.md) | The frozen snapshot: all 35 pages as they were at the end of Lesson 5 (commit `7347c27`), shown by the viewer's **Wireframe** mode |

The viewer has a **Wireframe / Design** toggle beside "Open as a page". **Design** (the default) shows the working screens in `wireframes/`, which Lessons 6 and 7 update in place. **Wireframe** shows the frozen Lesson 5 snapshot. The mode is in the URL (`#race-browse?mode=wireframe`, also through the workspace: `workspace.html#04/race-browse?mode=wireframe`) and survives switching screens and states. The snapshot is frozen history, not a copy to maintain.

The pages are wired along `flows.md`'s six flows (sign-in gate, catalog link, email confirmation, sign-out, password reset), so every path can be clicked through. Color, brand type and content icons are deferred to `concept/`.

## Concept

The visual concept is **Podium**: graphite ground, one scarce sodium-amber accent, Schibsted Grotesk for the title and key numbers, Hanken Grotesk for everything else, small radii, Solar bold icons and black-and-white race photos. Every value lives in a token, and every token is signed on the stand with its reason.

- **Stand:** [`concept/concept.html`](https://juliamakedonska.github.io/Onrace/concept/concept.html): color, type, shape, photos, icons and components, the color rules checked by the build, and every contrast pair.
- **Screens in Podium:** [Race browse](https://juliamakedonska.github.io/Onrace/wireframes/race-browse.html) and [Results list](https://juliamakedonska.github.io/Onrace/wireframes/results-list.html), with all their states. The other 8 screens stay grayscale until Lesson 7.
- **Rules and decisions:** [`concept/concept.md`](./concept/concept.md). References and photo sources: [`concept/references.md`](./concept/references.md).
